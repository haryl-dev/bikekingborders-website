About Bike King Borders

Your trusted cycling partner in the Scottish Borders

Who We Are

Bike King Borders is a growing cycling company located in the heart of the Scottish Borders. We specialise in high-quality bikes, professional servicing, and reliable bike hire for all levels of riders.

Whether you're a local rider or a tourist visiting the area, we aim to provide everything you need to enjoy the incredible trails right on our doorstep.



Our Mission

Our goal is to make cycling accessible, enjoyable, and exciting for everyone. From beginners to experienced riders, we’re here to support your journey.
